Submit your early-stage project.提交你的早期项目。

Takes about 10 minutes. Free. You can write in English or Chinese. Idea-stage, MVP and early-revenue software projects are all welcome.大约 10 分钟即可完成，免费，中英文填写均可。想法阶段、MVP 与早期收入阶段的软件项目都欢迎。

What happens next接下来会发生什么

  1. The operator reads your submission and replies by email (or WeChat, if you left it).运营者阅读你的提交，并通过邮件（如留了微信，也可能通过微信）回复你。
  2. We verify what can be verified — traction evidence, ownership, identity — by screenshots or screen-share. Never send passwords or API keys.我们通过截图或屏幕共享核验能核验的部分——数据证据、所有权、身份。请勿发送密码或 API 密钥。
  3. Together we write an anonymised, bilingual investor memo. Nothing is published until you approve it.我们与你一起撰写匿名的中英文投资备忘录。未经你确认，不会发布任何内容。
  4. When a reviewed investor asks for an intro, we ask you first. If you agree, we introduce you by email — then you talk directly, offline.有经审核的投资人申请引荐时，我们会先征求你的意见；你同意后，我们通过邮件引荐，之后双方在线下直接沟通。

What ProofMarket will not doProofMarket 不做什么

  • Raise, collect or hold money for you, or let anyone invest through this site.不替你募集、收取或保管资金，也不允许任何人通过本站投资。
  • Publish offering terms (price, valuation, share of the company) or promise investors any return.不发布发行条款（价格、估值、股权比例），也不向投资人承诺任何回报。
  • Show your profile to the general public as an investment opportunity — full details go only to reviewed investors you agree to meet.不把你的项目作为投资机会向社会公众推介——完整信息只提供给你同意会面的已审核投资人。

Please get your own legal advice before agreeing any investment terms. Questions first? Email 在商定任何投资条款前，请先咨询你自己的律师。想先问问？发邮件至 thaigore@gmail.com
