Takes about 5 minutes. During the pilot, the first sellers get a free verified listing. You can write in English or Chinese.大约 5 分钟即可完成。试点期间，首批卖家可免费获得核验挂牌。中英文填写均可。

What happens next接下来会发生什么

  1. The operator reads your application and replies by email (and WeChat, if you left it).运营者阅读你的申请，并通过邮件（如留了微信，也可能通过微信）回复你。
  2. We ask for ownership and revenue evidence — screen-share or exports. Never send passwords or API keys.我们会请你提供所有权与收入证明——屏幕共享或数据导出均可。请勿发送密码或 API 密钥。
  3. We write a verification note and help prepare a clear bilingual listing for overseas buyers.我们撰写核验说明，并协助准备面向海外买家的清晰中英文挂牌。
  4. Your listing goes live for 90 days; we introduce interested buyers. You agree terms and transfer directly — no escrow by ProofMarket.挂牌上线 90 天，我们为你介绍感兴趣的买家。双方直接谈判与交割——ProofMarket 不托管资金。
