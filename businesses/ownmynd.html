Problem痛点

People save notes, screenshots, links and social posts across many apps, then struggle to find them again.人们把笔记、截图、链接和社交内容存在许多应用里，之后却很难再找到。

Product产品

OwnMynd AI is a live iOS and iPadOS app. The public site and App Store page describe it as a private AI memory store: save notes, screenshots, PDFs, links, camera captures, and social posts or reels, then ask in plain language and receive an answer that cites the original saved item. The public site also describes an iOS Share Extension for saving from other apps, including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Safari. The seller separately stated that users can share from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and their photos.OwnMynd AI 是已上架的 iOS 和 iPadOS 应用。公开站点和 App Store 页面把它说明为私密的 AI 记忆库：保存笔记、截图、PDF、链接、相机拍摄以及社交帖或短视频，再用日常语言提问，并得到引用原始条目的回答。公开站点还描述了 iOS 分享扩展，可从 Instagram、TikTok、YouTube 和 Safari 等应用保存内容。卖家另外写明，用户可以从 Instagram、TikTok、YouTube 和相册分享。

The public site and App Store text describe a local-first design, with saved content stored on the device and optional cloud AI that, according to those pages, receives text needed for summaries or answers rather than raw images or original files. The App Store privacy label, retrieved on 25 Nov 2026, states that contact info, user content, and identifiers may be collected and linked to the user's identity. Those are public disclosures, not an independent privacy audit.公开站点和 App Store 文案描述为本地优先：保存的内容放在设备上，可选的云端 AI 按这些页面的说法只会接收生成摘要或回答所需的文本，而不是原始图片或原文件。2026-11-25 检索的 App Store 隐私标签写明，联系信息、用户内容和标识符可能会被收集并关联到用户身份。这些是公开披露，不是独立的隐私审计。

The public site says a desktop companion for Mac and Windows is in the works and is not yet available.公开站点写明 Mac 和 Windows 桌面配套应用仍在筹备，尚未上线。
