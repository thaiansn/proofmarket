Students, teachers and professionals still spend too long turning documents into slide decks. Formatting eats time that should go to content.学生、老师和职场人仍要花大量时间把文档整理成幻灯片，排版占掉本该用在内容上的时间。

Product产品

PPT Fusion is a live web app: upload a document, pick a theme, optionally edit in-browser (paid plans), then download a structured .pptx. Accounts, dashboard, presentation history and a brand kit (logo, colours, fonts) are built in. Free / Pro / Team plans; the seller states Paystack subscriptions (checkout, renewals, cancellations, plan switching) are wired, plus an optional OpenAI slide planner with a built-in fallback when AI is off.PPT Fusion 已上线：上传文档、选主题、（付费档）可在浏览器内改稿，再下载结构化 .pptx。已具备账号、仪表盘、演示历史与品牌套件（logo、配色、字体）。分 Free / Pro / Team 三档；卖家表示已接通 Paystack 订阅（结账、续费、取消、换档），并有可选的 OpenAI 幻灯片规划器，关闭 AI 时有内置回退。

Plan prices seen on the live pricing page in the seller's recording: Free ₦0/month, Pro ₦5,000/month, Team ₦12,000/month (Nigerian naira).卖家录屏中 pricing 页面显示的价格：Free ₦0/月、Pro ₦5,000/月、Team ₦12,000/月（尼日利亚奈拉）。
