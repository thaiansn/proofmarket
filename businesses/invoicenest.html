Owner workload业主工作量

Most weeks: 2–3 hours on support tickets and invoice edge cases, plus ~1 hour reviewing failed payments. Monthly: 2 hours on tax export and a short changelog. No sales calls; acquisition is SEO + word of mouth.多数周：2–3 小时处理支持工单与开票边界情况，另约 1 小时查看失败付款。每月：2 小时税务导出与简短更新日志。无销售通话；获客靠 SEO 与口碑。

Customers & retention客户与留存

412 active subscribers. Top 10 customers = 18% of MRR. Monthly logo churn ~3.2%. Net revenue retention ≈104% (small upgrades to multi-seat). Customers are solo consultants and 2–5 person studios in US/EU/AU.412 名活跃订阅者。前 10 大客户占 MRR 的 18%。月 logo 流失约 3.2%。净收入留存约 104%（少量多席位升级）。客户为美国/欧洲/澳洲的独立顾问与 2–5 人工作室。

Seller story卖家故事

I built InvoiceNest after sending my own invoices from a messy spreadsheet. It grew slowly through consultant communities. I’m selling to focus on a full-time role and don’t want a neglected product. The codebase is intentionally small — features that don’t reduce invoice friction were cut.我在用混乱表格开票后做出了 InvoiceNest。它通过顾问社区缓慢增长。出售是为了投入全职工作，不愿让产品荒废。代码库刻意保持精简——不能降低开票摩擦的功能都砍掉了。
