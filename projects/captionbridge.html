1. Problem1. 要解决的问题

Creators who want to publish in a second language face two bad options: human subtitling at roughly $3–8 per minute, or raw machine translation that mangles slang, names and on-screen context. Built-in platform auto-translation is hard to review or correct at scale, so many creators simply don't localise.想用第二语言发布内容的创作者面临两个糟糕选项：每分钟约 3–8 美元的人工字幕，或会把俚语、人名和画面语境翻错的机器直译。平台自带的自动翻译难以大批量审校修改，所以很多创作者干脆不做本地化。

2. Solution2. 解决方案

Upload a video → CaptionBridge drafts bilingual subtitles using the creator's own glossary (names, catchphrases, product terms) → a side-by-side review editor flags low-confidence lines → export SRT or burned-in video. An optional AI-dub preview is in beta. The glossary is reused on every upload, so quality improves per creator.上传视频 → CaptionBridge 结合创作者自己的术语表（人名、口头禅、产品名）生成双语字幕初稿 → 并排审校编辑器标出低置信度的句子 → 导出 SRT 或烧录字幕的视频。AI 配音预览处于测试阶段。术语表每次上传都会复用，因此每位创作者的翻译质量会越用越好。

3. Market & competition3. 市场与竞争

Investor's view: the target is mid-sized creators (roughly 10k–500k followers) who already earn from overseas audiences, starting with Chinese ↔ English. The space is crowded — general AI dubbing and editing tools, plus the platforms' own translation features — so the bet is on the review workflow and per-creator glossary as a wedge, not on the underlying model. Founder's market-size estimate has not been verified and is not shown.投资人视角：目标用户是已经从海外观众获得收入的中型创作者（约 1 万–50 万粉丝），先从中英互译切入。这个赛道很拥挤——通用 AI 配音/剪辑工具以及平台自带的翻译功能——因此押注点在于审校流程和“每位创作者专属术语表”这一切入口，而不是底层模型。创始人提供的市场规模估算未经核验，故不展示。

7. Key risks7. 主要风险

  • Platform risk: video platforms keep improving built-in translation and dubbing.平台风险：视频平台持续改进自带的翻译与配音功能。
  • AI API prices and terms can change and directly affect margin.AI 接口价格与条款可能变化，直接影响毛利。
  • Customer concentration: top 10 customers ≈ 22% of MRR (founder-reported).客户集中度：前 10 名客户约占月收入 22%（创始人自报）。
  • Two-person team; key-person risk.两人团队，存在关键人风险。
  • Legal entity, IP ownership and cross-border structure must be checked by the investor's own counsel.法律主体、知识产权归属与跨境架构需由投资人自己的律师核查。

8. Questions an investor should ask8. 投资人应追问的问题

  • Why will a review workflow beat fully automatic dubbing as models improve?随着模型进步，为什么审校流程能胜过全自动配音？
  • What is net revenue retention by cohort, and how is expansion priced?各批次的净收入留存是多少？增购如何定价？
  • Customer acquisition cost by channel — what is repeatable?各渠道获客成本是多少？哪些渠道可复制？
  • Where are the legal entity and the IP held today?目前的法律主体和知识产权归属在哪里？
