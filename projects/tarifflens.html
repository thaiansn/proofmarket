1. Problem1. 要解决的问题

Small cross-border sellers often guess customs classification codes. Wrong codes cause parcels to be held, unexpected duties for buyers and refunds for sellers. Customs brokers are too expensive for low-value parcels, and rule changes in major markets have made the problem more frequent.中小跨境卖家常常凭感觉填写海关商品编码。编码错误会导致包裹被扣、买家被收取意外关税、卖家被迫退款。报关行对低货值包裹来说太贵，而主要市场的规则变化让这个问题越来越频繁。

2. Solution2. 解决方案

Paste a product listing or photo → TariffLens suggests a classification code with its reasoning and a confidence level, estimates duty and VAT for the US, EU and UK, and exports the data for a commercial invoice. Low-confidence items are flagged "ask a licensed broker" rather than guessed.粘贴商品详情或上传图片 → TariffLens 给出建议的商品编码、判断理由与置信度，预估美国、欧盟、英国的关税与增值税，并导出商业发票所需数据。低置信度的商品会被标注“请咨询持牌报关行”，而不是硬猜。

3. Market & competition3. 市场与竞争

Investor's view: a large number of small sellers ship cross-border, and recent rule changes increased the need for correct paperwork. Willingness to pay is unproven (no pricing yet), and accuracy carries liability risk, so the key diligence items are accuracy against a broker baseline and how disclaimers and human escalation work.投资人视角：大量中小卖家在做跨境发货，近期的规则变化提高了对单证准确性的需求。付费意愿尚未验证（还没有定价），而准确率又牵涉责任风险，因此尽调重点是：与报关行基准相比的准确率，以及免责声明与人工兜底机制如何运作。

7. Key risks7. 主要风险

  • Accuracy and liability: wrong suggestions can cost users money.准确率与责任：错误建议可能让用户蒙受损失。
  • No revenue yet; willingness to pay unproven.尚无收入，付费意愿未验证。
  • Co-founder is part-time.联合创始人目前为兼职。
  • Customs rules change frequently in every market.各市场海关规则变化频繁。
  • Competition from broker software and marketplace tools.面临报关软件与电商平台自带工具的竞争。

8. Questions an investor should ask8. 投资人应追问的问题

  • How is accuracy measured, and against what baseline?准确率如何衡量？以什么为基准？
  • Who pays — sellers, logistics partners or marketplaces?谁来付费——卖家、物流合作方还是平台？
  • What do the disclaimers and human-escalation flow look like in practice?免责声明和人工兜底流程实际如何运作？
