About关于

A careful bridge between indie builders and buyers.连接独立开发者与买家的一座谨慎的桥。

Thousands of small software products — SaaS tools, AI wrappers, mobile apps, templates, newsletters — earn real money but never find a buyer when their makers move on. Many are built by Chinese-speaking indie developers whose customers are overseas, while the buyers who want them speak English and cannot easily judge what they are looking at.大量小型软件产品——SaaS 工具、AI 应用、手机 App、模板、Newsletter——在真实赚钱，却在开发者想转向时找不到买家。其中很多由中文独立开发者打造，客户在海外；而想收购的买家说英文，很难判断这些项目的真实情况。

ProofMarket closes that gap with a deliberately manual process: an operator checks ownership and revenue evidence, writes down exactly what was verified, presents the business clearly in both languages, and introduces serious buyers.ProofMarket 用刻意保持人工的流程来弥合这一差距：运营者核对所有权和收入证明，写清楚到底核验了什么，用中英双语清晰呈现项目，并介绍认真的买家。

Principles原则

  • Clarity before commitment — revenue, costs, workload and risks first.承诺之前，先看清楚——先讲收入、成本、工作量和风险。
  • Say what was checked — every listing has a written verification note.说清楚核验了什么——每个挂牌都有书面核验说明。
  • Examples stay labeled — we never blur fictional examples and real listings.示例始终标注——绝不混淆虚构示例与真实挂牌。
  • We never touch the money — no escrow; buyers and sellers transact directly.不碰交易资金——不托管，买卖双方直接交易。

Status现状

ProofMarket is an early pilot. There are no verified live listings yet; the six listings on the homepage are fictional examples. The first sellers are verified and listed free. No payments are taken on this site.ProofMarket 目前处于早期试点阶段，尚无已核验的真实挂牌；首页的六个挂牌均为虚构示例。首批卖家免费核验与挂牌。本站不收取任何付款。
