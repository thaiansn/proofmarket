How selling works出售流程

Apply提交申请

Tell us about the business: revenue, profit, asking price and what proof you can show. Applying is free.填写项目信息：收入、利润、要价，以及你能提供的收入证明。申请免费。

Manual verification人工核验

The operator reviews the evidence you can show (product recordings, ownership, revenue dashboards if any), then writes a verification note stating what was checked, for which period, and what was not checked.运营者查看你能提供的证据（产品录屏、所有权、如有收入则看收入后台），并撰写核验说明，写明核对了什么、覆盖哪个时间段、哪些没核对。

Live for 90 days挂牌 90 天

Your listing and its verification note are published for 90 days. No automatic renewal.挂牌与核验说明公开展示 90 天，不会自动续期。

Introductions人工牵线

Buyers share budget and questions; the operator introduces suitable buyers manually. You agree terms and transfer directly — no escrow.买家提交预算与问题，运营者人工介绍合适的买家。双方直接谈判、交割——平台不托管资金。
