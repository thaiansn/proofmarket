The service服务内容

ProofMarket publishes manually reviewed profiles of small software businesses and arranges introductions between sellers and buyers. It is not a party to any sale, is not a broker, escrow agent or financial adviser, and does not receive or hold acquisition funds. ProofMarket also shows anonymised profiles of early-stage projects and makes manual introductions between their founders and reviewed investors, under the additional terms below.ProofMarket 发布经人工审核的小型软件项目介绍，并为买卖双方安排对接。ProofMarket 不是任何交易的当事方，不是经纪人、托管方或财务顾问，也不接收或持有收购资金。ProofMarket 另外展示早期项目的匿名介绍，并在创始人与经审核的投资人之间进行人工引荐，适用下文的补充条款。
