How the early-stage track works早期项目如何运作

Founder submits创始人提交

The founder sends the project through the founder form. Free.创始人通过创始人表单提交项目，免费。

Verification人工核验

The operator checks what can be checked — traction evidence, ownership, identity — and marks each metric verified, partly verified or founder-reported.运营者核对能核对的内容——数据证据、所有权、身份——并把每项指标标注为已核验、部分核验或创始人自报。

Anonymised profile匿名展示

An investor-memo style profile is published without founder names, contact details or sensitive data. The founder approves the text first.以投资备忘录形式发布匿名项目介绍，不含创始人姓名、联系方式或敏感数据；发布前由创始人确认文稿。

Investor applies投资人申请

Investors apply, self-declare qualified / professional investor status and are reviewed by the operator.投资人提交申请、自我声明合格/专业投资者身份，并由运营者审核。

Manual introduction人工引荐

When a reviewed investor asks for an intro and the founder agrees, the operator introduces them by email.经审核的投资人提出引荐请求、且创始人同意后，运营者通过邮件为双方引荐。

Direct, offline talks双方线下直接洽谈

Due diligence, any terms, documents and money are handled directly between the parties and their own advisers — never on ProofMarket.尽调、任何条款、文件与资金，均由双方及各自顾问直接处理——绝不经过 ProofMarket。
