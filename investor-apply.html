Investors · Early-stage投资人 · 早期项目

Apply as an investor.申请成为投资人。

For qualified / professional investors only. Profiles on ProofMarket are anonymised summaries; after review you can request introductions, and full materials and founder contact are shared only when the founder agrees.仅面向合格 / 专业投资者。ProofMarket 上的项目页面为匿名摘要；通过审核后可申请引荐，完整资料与创始人联系方式仅在创始人同意后提供。

This page is not an invitation to the general public to invest. ProofMarket does not raise money, does not take investments and does not promise returns.本页不是向社会公众发出的投资邀请。ProofMarket 不募集资金、不接受投资、不承诺任何回报。

How it works流程

  1. Submit this application. The operator reviews it and may ask a few questions by email or a short call.提交本申请。运营者进行审核，可能通过邮件或简短通话问几个问题。
  2. Once reviewed, you can request an intro to a specific project.通过审核后，你可以申请引荐某个具体项目。
  3. The founder decides. If they agree, the operator introduces you both by email and shares the full materials.由创始人决定。创始人同意后，运营者通过邮件为双方引荐并提供完整资料。
  4. You do your own due diligence and negotiate directly with the founder, offline, with your own advisers. Money never passes through ProofMarket.你自行尽调，并在线下、在你自己的顾问协助下与创始人直接洽谈。资金绝不经过 ProofMarket。

Good to know须知

  • Every metric on a profile is marked verified, partly verified or founder-reported. Verification is not an audit, valuation or endorsement.项目页上的每项指标都标注为已核验、部分核验或创始人自报。核验不构成审计、估值或背书。
  • Early-stage investments are high-risk and illiquid; you may lose all of the money you put in.早期投资风险高、流动性差，可能损失全部投入资金。
  • The projects currently shown are fictional examples; real profiles will be shared with reviewed investors first.目前展示的项目均为虚构示例；真实项目会优先提供给已审核的投资人。
