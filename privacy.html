What we collect我们收集什么

  • Seller applications: name, email, optional WeChat ID, project name and URL, category, revenue, profit, asking price, type of revenue proof, notes and your consent.卖家申请：姓名、邮箱、选填的微信号、项目名称与网址、类别、收入、利润、期望售价、收入证明类型、补充说明及你的同意。
  • Buyer interest: name, email, budget range, categories of interest, notes and your consent.买家意向：姓名、邮箱、预算范围、感兴趣的类别、补充说明及你的同意。
  • Emails you send us, and evidence you choose to show during verification.你发给我们的邮件，以及你在核验中自愿出示的证明材料。

There are no accounts, logins, advertising trackers or analytics on this site. Your language choice is stored only in your browser (localStorage).本站没有账号、登录、广告追踪或分析统计。你的语言偏好只保存在你自己的浏览器中（localStorage）。

How forms are delivered表单如何传送

When you submit the seller or buyer form, the data you enter is sent to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form-processing service, which processes it and delivers it to the operator’s email address (thaigore@gmail.com). If the form cannot be sent, you can instead send the same details from your own email app (prefilled email). Our mailbox is hosted by 你提交卖家或买家表单时，所填写的内容会发送至第三方表单处理服务 Formspree（formspree.io），由其处理后转发到运营者的邮箱（thaigore@gmail.com）。如果表单无法发送，你也可以改用自己的邮件应用（预填邮件）发送同样的信息。我们的邮箱服务商为 Google (Gmail)Google（Gmail）. These providers may process data outside your country.这些服务商可能在你所在国家/地区以外处理数据。

How we use it我们如何使用

To review applications, verify listings, publish approved listings, match buyers with listings, arrange introductions and reply to you. Contact details are never shown on public listings. We share a buyer’s or seller’s details with the other side only for an introduction you asked for or agreed to. We do not sell personal data.用于审核申请、核验挂牌、发布已通过的挂牌、为买家匹配挂牌、安排对接以及回复你。联系方式不会出现在公开挂牌中。只有在你请求或同意对接时，我们才会把你的信息提供给另一方。我们不出售个人数据。

Retention and your rights保存期限与你的权利

We keep submissions while needed for the pilot and delete them on request, unless we must keep a record by law. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.我们在试点所需期间保存提交内容，并会应你的要求删除，法律要求保留的除外。你可以发邮件要求查阅、更正或删除你的数据。

Governing law适用法律

This policy is governed by the laws of the People’s Republic of China. If a dispute arises, we will first try to resolve it in good faith by email; if that fails, it may be submitted to a competent court at the operator’s location (Tianjin, China), unless mandatory law gives you the right to another forum.本政策适用中华人民共和国法律。如发生争议，双方应首先通过邮件友好协商解决；协商不成的，可提交运营方所在地（中国·天津）有管辖权的人民法院处理，但法律强制性规定另有管辖的除外。
