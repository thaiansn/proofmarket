When you submit any form on this site (seller, buyer, early-stage founder or investor), the data you enter is sent to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form-processing service, which processes it and delivers it to the operator’s email address (thaigore@gmail.com). If the form cannot be sent, you can instead send the same details from your own email app (prefilled email). How we use it我们如何使用

To review applications, verify listings, publish approved listings, match buyers with listings, arrange introductions and reply to you. Contact details are never shown on public listings. We share a buyer’s or seller’s details with the other side only for an introduction you asked for or agreed to. Early-stage project profiles are published only in anonymised form and only after the founder approves the text; a founder’s identity, contact details and full materials are shared only with a reviewed investor the founder has agreed to be introduced to, and an investor’s details are shared only with founders for intros the investor requested. We do not sell personal data.用于审核申请、核验挂牌、发布已通过的挂牌、为买家匹配挂牌、安排对接以及回复你。联系方式不会出现在公开挂牌中。只有在你请求或同意对接时，我们才会把你的信息提供给另一方。早期项目仅以匿名形式、并在创始人确认文稿后发布；创始人的身份、联系方式与完整资料，只会提供给创始人同意引荐的已审核投资人；投资人的信息也只会提供给其申请引荐的创始人。我们不出售个人数据。

Retention and your rights保存期限与你的权利

We keep submissions while needed for the pilot and delete them on request, unless we must keep a record by law. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.我们在试点所需期间保存提交内容，并会应你的要求删除，法律要求保留的除外。你可以发邮件要求查阅、更正或删除你的数据。
